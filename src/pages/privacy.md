**Last updated: August 15, 2026**

## Overview

Piano Fitness is designed to be useful without an account. This policy explains
the limited information involved when you use the Piano Fitness website, app,
or public project spaces.

## The app

The app stores profiles, settings, and practice history locally on your device.
Piano Fitness does not require an account, and the app does not include its own
analytics or advertising service.

If you download the app through an app store, the store provider may process
information under its own privacy policy. We may receive aggregate reports from
the store, such as downloads, sales, refunds, or broadly aggregated usage
metrics. These reports are not a substitute for individual user records.

## The website

The Piano Fitness website does not include analytics or advertising tracking
code. Its hosting provider may process basic request information, such as IP
address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. This information
is handled according to the provider's applicable policies and configuration.

## Contact and public contributions

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send so that we can
reply. If you use GitHub to open an issue, comment, or contribute code, your
GitHub username and the information you post are public and governed by
GitHub's terms and privacy policy.

## How we use information

We use the limited information described above to respond to messages, maintain
the project, understand aggregate app-store performance, and keep the website
secure. We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

You can delete local app data from your device. For information you provide by
email, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
For information posted on GitHub or handled by an app store, use that service's
own privacy controls and request process.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or its data practices change. The
date above will indicate the latest revision.

For privacy questions, contact [privacy@piano.fitness](mailto:privacy@piano.fitness).
